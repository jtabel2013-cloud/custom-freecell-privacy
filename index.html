Privacy Policy — Custom Freecell +

Effective Date: October 19, 2026

Custom Freecell + is an Android card game. This policy explains how information is handled.

Information Stored on Your Device

The game stores saved progress, gameplay statistics, customization settings, and preferences locally on your device. No account is required to play.

Advertising and Third-Party Services

Custom Freecell + uses Google AdMob for banner advertisements and rewarded advertisements, including hints and additional undos.

Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, advertising identifiers where available, IP addresses, approximate location inferred from network information, advertising interactions, device information, and diagnostic data for advertising, measurement, security, and fraud prevention.

See Google Privacy Policy and Google AdMob Privacy Information.

Use and Sharing of Information

Locally stored game information supports gameplay and preferences. The game does not require a user account or intentionally transmit locally stored game records to the developer. Advertising-related information may be processed and shared by Google and its advertising partners according to their policies.

Data Retention and Deletion

Local game data generally remains until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Advertising providers manage their own data retention and deletion practices.

Children's Privacy

Custom Freecell + is not specifically directed toward children. Applicable age-related privacy and advertising requirements apply.

Security

The game relies on Android application storage protections for locally saved game data. No electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Policy Updates

This policy may change if the game's features, services, or data-handling practices change. Updates will be posted here.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer using the public support email listed on the game's Google Play page.
